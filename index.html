About

I am a Postdoctoral Associate at MIT Sloan School of Management and the Schwarzman College of Computing's SERC program, working with Prof. Rahul Mazumder and Prof. Suzanne Flynn.

My research develops interpretable machine learning methods and statistical inference tools with applications in healthcare, finance, and public policy. Current focus areas include explainable AI, differential privacy, and Rashomon inference.

Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Auburn University with Prof. Roberto Molinari, where I developed the DP-TOST framework for differentially private equivalence testing and led an NIH R21 grant proposal. Before that, I was a Research Officer & Manager at the Reserve Bank of India. I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay.
